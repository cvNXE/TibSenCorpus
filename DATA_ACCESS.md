# TibSenCorpus: Non-commercial Research Access

## Scope

TibSenCorpus is made available through controlled password distribution for non-commercial research. Downloading the encrypted archive does not itself grant permission to use or redistribute the underlying data.

## Requesting access

1. Download the blank [TibSenCorpus Dataset Release Agreement](agreements/TibSenCorpus_Dataset_Release_Agreement.pdf).
2. Type your name, affiliation, institutional email, and research purpose into the form.
3. Print the one-page agreement and sign by hand. Student applicants must also obtain their supervisor's or principal investigator's signature. Other applicants may act as the responsible researcher themselves.
4. Scan the complete signed page into a legible PDF and email it as an attachment to **cvlina@mail.nwpu.edu.cn** with the subject **TibSenCorpus Data Access Request**. Use your institutional email; if none is available, contact the maintainer before submitting.
5. After reviewing the signed application, the maintainer will send the archive password if access is approved.

An email-only statement is not a complete application. The signed agreement, not the email template, records acceptance of the access conditions. **Do not upload completed agreements to this public repository or its issues.**

## Conditions of access

The signed agreement is the full access document. In summary, applicants agree to:

1. Use TibSenCorpus only for non-commercial research, not for commercial products, commercial services, sale, or resale.
2. Keep the extraction password, unencrypted data, subsets, and record-level copies within the approved research project. Do not publish, re-upload, sell, or distribute them to third parties. Other research groups should request access independently.
3. Protect sensitive content when storing, processing, or reporting on the data. Do not publish sensitive source passages or identifying information from the corpus.
4. Acknowledge TibSenCorpus and link to this repository in research outputs that use the dataset.

The labels are research annotations, not legal determinations or grounds for unreviewed decisions about individuals. Source-text rights remain with their respective rights holders; these access terms do not transfer third-party rights or authorize uses beyond the stated research purpose.

Preprocessing, tokenization, training, evaluation, and internal research copies are permitted for the approved non-commercial project. Papers, code, aggregate statistics, and visualizations may be shared without disclosing or enabling reconstruction of restricted records. The agreement does not ban ordinary research preprocessing.

## Basis of this access procedure

This procedure adapts publicly documented practices rather than adopting another dataset's licence:

- [SUSTech1K release agreement](https://lidargait.github.io/static/resources/SUSTech1KAgreement.pdf): researcher information, signatures, and submission of a scanned agreement by email.
- [EPHOIE](https://github.com/HCIILAB/EPHOIE): a signed application followed by review and email delivery of access information.
- [DocTamper](https://github.com/qcf-568/DocTamper) and [VisualSem](https://github.com/iacercalixto/visualsem): non-commercial research and email-based password requests.

These projects do not endorse TibSenCorpus. No Creative Commons, MIT, or other third-party dataset licence is implied by these references.
